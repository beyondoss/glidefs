Scatter (from boot_profile, boot set vs image pack layout):
  busybox     : 1 of 1 packs
  nginx       : 2 of 2 packs
  python_slim : 1 of 1 packs (but 4 readahead GETs — within-pack scatter)
  node_slim   : 2 of 2 packs (9 readahead GETs — within-pack scatter)
  python_full : 7 of 9 packs (complete boot set, 90s profile)
  node_full   : 5 of 9 packs (TRUNCATED at 90s timeout — conservative; real set larger)

Notes:
- node_full boot trace was cut off by the profile timeout (node cold-boots slowly through
  ublk→S3); its 16.9× is therefore a LOWER bound.
- boot-set MiB < boot set MiB because the reordered run is re-compressed; readahead rd_MiB is
  the actual S3 transfer (compressed) and is dominated by over-fetch of non-boot blocks.
