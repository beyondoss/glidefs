Change
==============================================================================

glidefs/src/block/ublk/device.rs:38
  const IO_BUF_BYTES: u32 = 512 * 1024  ->  128 * 1024

Rationale: glidefs's WriteCache block size is 128 KB; the kernel splits any
larger upstream bio into 128 KB chunks at max_sectors_kb (which is pinned
via max_io_buf_bytes). The 512 KB ceiling was over-spec — cited "room for
large I/Os" with no measurement. Cutting it 4× cuts per-tag RSS by the same
factor at any device density.

Verdict
==============================================================================

Step 1 lands. The 3-4% throughput cost is the kernel doing 4× more I/O
round trips on bios > 128 KB; it is within the planned 5% gate.

Projected RSS at 5k devices: 21 GiB warm → ~5 GiB warm. Single largest
RSS win available without a protocol change. Next: Step 2 (histogram
instrument) and Step 3 (density stress curve).
